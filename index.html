The Library Collection

A curated compendium of landmark literary works from distinct heritages.

Old English Classics

Beowulf — Anonymous

An epic Anglo-Saxon heroic poem tracking the legendary geatish warrior Beowulf as he fights the monster Grendel, Grendel's vengeful mother, and a devastating fire-breathing dragon.

The Canterbury Tales — Geoffrey Chaucer

A vibrant collection of stories told by a diverse group of pilgrims traveling together to Canterbury Cathedral, serving as an unmatched, witty study of medieval society and human nature.

Sir Gawain and the Green Knight — Anonymous

A classic chivalric romance tracking King Arthur's noble nephew, Sir Gawain, on an unsettling quest to fulfill a deadly pact made with a mysterious, immortal green warrior.

Western Literature & Pop-Culture

The Great Gatsby — F. Scott Fitzgerald

Set during the glamorous Roaring Twenties, this landmark novel critiques the elusive American Dream through the obsessive, tragic love affair of a self-made millionaire named Jay Gatsby.

Pride and Prejudice — Jane Austen

A dazzling romantic comedy of manners that highlights the turbulent relationship between the brilliant Elizabeth Bennet and the wealthy, proud aristocratic landowner Mr. Darcy.

1984 — George Orwell

A chilling dystopian masterpiece exploring total governmental oversight, psychological manipulation, and the tragic erasure of historical truth under an all-seeing dictator known as Big Brother.

Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire — J.K. Rowling

The pivotal fourth year at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, where Harry is unexpectedly bound to face dangerous tasks in the iconic, magical Triwizard Tournament.

Filipino Literary Treasures

Noli Me Tángere — Dr. José Rizal

The foundational socio-political novel of the Philippines exposing the rampant corruption, inequalities, and deep systemic abuses under Spanish colonial clerical rule, sparking a cultural revolution.

El Filibusterismo — Dr. José Rizal

The dark, suspenseful sequel to Noli Me Tángere, capturing a radical, bitter return via an alias to plot an underground revolution to overthrow oppressive structures.

Dekada '70 — Lualhati Bautista

A poignant family drama showing a middle-class Manila family navigating the complex socio-political landscape, state restrictions, and personal transformations under Martial Law in the 1970s.

Trese — Budjette Tan & Kajo Baldisimo

A highly acclaimed, dark graphic novel series following detective Alexandra Trese as she solves gruesome criminal cases involving mythological creatures of underworld Filipino folklore hiding in modern Manila.

About Eunoia

Eunoia comes from a Greek word meaning “beautiful thinking” or “a well mind.” It represents positive, meaningful thoughts and a deep sense of understanding. This meaning connects to our website because Eunoia helps readers discover books that match their personalities, interests, and reading preferences, encouraging a more enjoyable and meaningful reading experience.

Eunoia is a website designed for readers who want to identify the types of books that best suit their interests and preferences. Many readers struggle to choose books that truly match their tastes, often leading to loss of interest in reading. To address this, the website provides a personalized and engaging way to help users discover books they are more likely to enjoy.

The website features 50 carefully designed yes-or-no questions that assess the user’s reading habits, interests, personality, and preferences. These questions aim to understand what kind of stories, themes, writing styles, and genres the user is naturally drawn to. After completing the questionnaire, the system will tally the responses and determine the user’s reader type based on their answers.

Once the user’s reader type is identified, Eunoia will generate personalized book recommendations tailored to their preferences. For example, users who enjoy mystery and suspense may be recommended thriller or detective novels, while those who prefer emotional and character-driven stories may receive romance or contemporary fiction suggestions. This personalized approach makes book selection easier, more enjoyable, and better suited to each reader’s unique interests.

Through Eunoia, readers are guided toward books that align with their preferences, helping them develop a deeper appreciation for reading while making the process of finding suitable books more convenient and enjoyable.
